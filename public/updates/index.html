The eight items in the official game description, with what each one means when you play. The game does not publish costs, odds, or effects, so we do not invent them.

Update 10 addition What it means for your farm

Update FAQ

Living with a changing game.

Short answers to the update questions players ask most.

What is the latest Chicken Farm update?

Update 10, live since September 5, 2026. It was still the latest changelog in the official description when we checked on October 1, 2026.

How do I get Gold Chickens?

The official changelog lists Gold Chickens but does not say how to get them, and we have not found a reliable written explanation. Watch your merge results and Lucky Block drops, and check the in-game index. We will add the method once it is documented.

Did Update 10 change rebirth costs?

It added a new rebirth and balance changes, so older community cost tables are out of date. The rebirth page covers what resets and what the Robux Skip keeps.

When is the next Chicken Farm update?

No date has been announced. Updates 8, 9, and 10 arrived within about two weeks of each other, and earlier updates were tied to Saturday Admin Abuse sessions. Since September 12 the events calendar has been empty, so we cannot predict a date.
