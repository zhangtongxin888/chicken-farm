Update FAQ

Living with a changing game.

How to read any future update without getting burned by stale info.

Is Update 7 the latest update?

It is the changelog listed in the official description as of our August 20, 2026 check. If the game page shows a newer entry when you read this, that newer entry wins.

Did Update 7 change rebirth costs?

It added two new rebirths and balance changes, so this guide does not reuse pre-update cost tables. Check the live rebirth screen; our rebirth page lists the reset and retention facts approved on August 20, 2026.

Where do I see Gem Upgrades?

They unlock at Rebirth 3. Once you reach it, the in-game menu is the only reliable source for costs and effects.

How often does this site re-check facts?

Every page carries its check date (currently August 20, 2026). Mutable details — prices, code status, update contents — should always be re-confirmed in-game regardless of what any guide says.
