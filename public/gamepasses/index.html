Chicken Farm gamepasses: all 4, with prices.

Chicken Farm sells four gamepasses: Auto Collect Money (29 Robux), Auto Collect Eggs (79), Double Eggs (499), and Double Gems (799), which was added on August 29, 2026 and only works from Rebirth 3. All four were on sale when we checked the Roblox game-pass API on October 1, 2026. Buying the full set costs 1,406 Robux.

Gamepass FAQ

Before you spend Robux.

Honest answers about value and necessity.

What does this page verify?

The four catalog names, pass IDs, product IDs, on-sale status, and prices observed on October 1, 2026, plus Double Gems' official description. The other three passes have empty official descriptions.

Which pass is the best value?

We do not rank them, because there are no official effect numbers. Two things are clear: Double Gems is useless before Rebirth 3, and the cheapest pass, Auto Collect Money at 29 Robux, is the smallest risk if you want to try one.

What does Double Gems do in Chicken Farm?

Its official description says it doubles the gems you gain and is unlocked at Rebirth 3. It went on sale on August 29, 2026 for 799 Robux, a week before Update 10 added more Gem Upgrades.

Can pass prices change?

Yes. Prices and sale status are mutable catalog data. This page was rechecked on October 1, 2026, so confirm the live price on the in-game or Roblox catalog page before purchasing.

Where should I confirm before buying?

Use the live in-game shop or Roblox catalog entry. This page keeps its October 1, 2026 check date visible so the snapshot is not mistaken for a permanent price.
