Gameplay

Playing the farm.

Deeper answers live on the linked system pages.

What do I do first in Chicken Farm?

Finish the short on-farm tutorial: walk over eggs from the yellow chicken building, deposit them, watch the brown processing board, then collect cash and buy chickens. The beginner guide walks it in five steps.

Do chickens lay eggs while I am offline?

Yes. The official description says chickens keep laying while you are away — offline production is an intended part of the loop, so always collect when you return.

How do I turn eggs into cash?

Walk over eggs to collect them, step on the Deposit Eggs pad to send them to the Egg Shop, wait for processing on the brown board, then step on the Collect Cash pad.

How do I get rarer chickens?

Merge up to 3 chickens at the Merge pad — rarer birds lay more precious eggs. Lucky Blocks are a second, random source. Full details on the merge page.

Is there a full chicken rarity list?

Not a verified one. Counts and rarities published before Update 7 may be stale, so use your in-game index as the reference.

Codes & progression

Boosts and resets.

The short version — with links to the full pages.

How do I redeem codes?

After the tutorial, open the ABX codes control on the top HUD, type the code exactly (it is case-sensitive), and press Redeem. Walkthrough on the codes page.

Which codes work right now?

We do not publish a list: the official description prints none, and same-day fan lists already disagree. Check in-game or the community Discord (discord.com/invite/chickfarm) for the current set.

What does a rebirth reset?

A normal rebirth resets cash, eggs, chickens, and Processing Level while raising the cash multiplier and unlocking extra Buy tiers or items. A Robux Skip advances while keeping those four reset items. Details on the rebirth page.

When do Gem Upgrades unlock?

At Rebirth 3, per the official Update 7 changelog (checked August 20, 2026). Gem costs and effects are not published — see them in-game.

What gamepasses exist and what do they cost?

Auto Collect Money (29 Robux), Auto Collect Eggs (79 Robux), and Double Eggs (499 Robux), rechecked against the official catalog on August 20, 2026. See the verified catalog IDs on the gamepasses page.
