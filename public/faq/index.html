Gameplay

Playing the farm.

Deeper answers live on the linked system pages.

What do I do first in Chicken Farm?

Finish the short on-farm tutorial: walk over eggs from the yellow chicken building, deposit them, watch the brown processing board, then collect cash and buy chickens. The beginner guide walks it in five steps.

Do chickens lay eggs while I am offline?

Yes. The official description says chickens keep laying while you are away — offline production is an intended part of the loop, so always collect when you return.

How do I turn eggs into cash?

Walk over eggs to collect them, step on the Deposit Eggs pad to send them to the Egg Shop, wait for processing on the brown board, then step on the Collect Cash pad.

How do I get rarer chickens?

Merge up to 3 chickens at the Merge pad — rarer birds lay more precious eggs. Lucky Blocks are a second, random source. Full details on the merge page.

Is there a full chicken rarity list?

Not a verified one. Update 10 added Gold Chickens and Lucky Blocks keep adding birds, so any older count is likely stale. Use your in-game index as the reference. The merge page covers what we can confirm.

Codes & progression

Boosts and resets.

The short version — with links to the full pages.

How do I redeem codes?

After the tutorial, click the ABX button at the top-left of the HUD, paste the code exactly as written, and press Redeem. Walkthrough on the codes page.

Which codes work right now?

As of October 1, 2026, 10 codes are listed as active by three separate trackers: update8, plsbesttier, adminarbuz, givemecash, toood, owemgeh, freecasssh!, 666klikes, 50MVisits, and THANKSFOR100K. Rewards and the expired list are on the codes page.

What does a rebirth reset?

A normal rebirth resets cash, eggs, chickens, and Processing Level while raising the cash multiplier and unlocking extra Buy tiers or items. A Robux Skip advances while keeping those four reset items. Details on the rebirth page.

When do Gem Upgrades unlock?

At Rebirth 3, per the official Update 7 changelog. Update 10 added more Gem Upgrades. Gem costs and effects are not published, so check them in-game. The Double Gems pass (799 Robux) is the only paid gem boost.

What gamepasses exist and what do they cost?

There are four: Auto Collect Money (29 Robux), Auto Collect Eggs (79 Robux), Double Eggs (499 Robux), and Double Gems (799 Robux), rechecked against the Roblox game-pass API on October 1, 2026. See the catalog IDs on the gamepasses page.

Is this the same as Chicken Farm Tycoon?

No. This guide covers Chicken Farm by Skandi Studios (place 137233438285284). Chicken Farm Tycoon by FunFirst Tycoons (place 10868496812) is a different experience — check the developer name before following any guide.

What is the Skandi Studios Discord link?

discord.gg/chickenfarm. On October 1, 2026 the Discord invite API resolved it to the server "Chicken Farm" with about 33,500 members. Many older guides still print discord.com/invite/chickfarm, which now returns "Unknown Invite". New codes and update news are posted in this server first.

Is there an official wiki or Trello?

We have not found an official wiki or Trello for this game. News comes from the official Discord (discord.gg/chickenfarm), the Skandi Studios Roblox group, and the changelog in the game description.

Do I get anything for joining the Skandi Studios group?

The Skandi Studios group (about 1.83M members) says "Join this group for in-game rewards!" but does not list the rewards. Joining is free. Group rewards are separate from codes, so you can claim both.
