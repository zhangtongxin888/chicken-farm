Step 01 · Learn the rhythm

Finish the on-farm tutorial

Eggs roll out of the yellow chicken building onto the ground — walk over them and they are collected automatically. Drop them on the Deposit Eggs pad to send them to the Egg Shop, watch the brown board on the right side of the farm track processing, then pick up your money on the Collect Cash pad. This is the opening loop described by the approved guides.

Step 02 · Grow the flock

Buy chickens in batches

Chickens are bought by stepping on pads: Buy 1, Buy 5, Buy 25, or Buy 100. Each pad deducts cash and places chickens on your farm.

Step 03 · Improve quality

Merge up to 3 chickens

Take up to 3 chickens to the Merge pad and combine them into one higher-quality bird. Approved guides say rarer chickens produce more precious eggs. Details and Lucky Block advice live on the merge page.

Step 04 · Optional boost

Redeem a code

Once the tutorial is done, the ABX codes control on the top HUD unlocks: type a code exactly as written — codes are case-sensitive — and press Redeem. No current code string or reward passed the approval gate, so this guide does not publish a code list. The verified walkthrough is on the codes page.

Step 05 · Look beyond minute ten

Set up for later

Your chickens keep laying while you are away, so it is fine to log off — collect the pile when you return. Skip paid Lucky Blocks until the loop feels automatic, and when rebirth appears, read what resets before confirming: cash, eggs, chickens, and Processing Level all wipe on a normal rebirth. Plan it on the rebirth page.

Beginner FAQ

First-session questions.

Quick answers that come up while working through the checklist.

Is everything done on my own farm?

Yes. Beginner guides confirm all early play happens on your own farm plot — there is no shared overworld to learn. Servers hold up to 6 players.

Should I buy Lucky Blocks early?

No. Lucky Blocks can cost a fee to open and reward a random chicken, so beginner guides advise spending starter cash on production chickens first. More on the merge page.

Is there a free reward for joining the group?

The Skandi Studios group page advertises in-game rewards for members, though the exact reward list is not specified. Joining is free and optional.

Which gamepasses are in the verified catalog?

The approved catalog records list Auto Collect Money (29 Robux), Auto Collect Eggs (79 Robux), and Double Eggs (499 Robux), checked August 20, 2026. See the dated IDs and prices on the gamepasses page.
