Beginner checklist

Your first 10 minutes on Chicken Farm.

Use this as a calm, in-order route through the approved basics. Tick each step as you go; your progress stays on this device.

Your farm setup 0 of 5 steps complete
Step 01 · Start the loop

Buy your first chicken

Use an in-game buy pad. Your first objective is not to memorize every possible chicken—it is to understand how a chicken starts the egg-to-cash loop.

Step 02 · Turn output into cash

Collect, then deposit

Follow the tutorial’s collection and deposit actions. This connects what your chickens produce to the cash you can put back into eggs and growth.

Step 03 · Improve the flock

Use the merge system

Move into the merge part of the tutorial and use it as your progression bridge. Then return to buying, producing, collecting, and reinvesting.

Step 04 · Find your rhythm

Repeat the loop once

Buy a chicken, collect and deposit eggs, merge upward, collect cash, then reinvest. One complete repeat makes the structure of Chicken Farm easier to recognize.

Step 05 · Look beyond minute ten

Plan, don’t rush, the next layer

Bring offline production and Lucky Blocks into your wider plan. When rebirth becomes relevant, read the current reset summary in-game before making the choice.

Returning later?

Offline production is part of progression. Collect what your farm produced and bring it back into the main loop.

Found a Lucky Block?

Treat Lucky Blocks as a side system that supports progression, not a replacement for understanding the core loop.

Thinking about rebirth?

Pause at the confirmation screen and read what will reset. Avoid relying on a static cost table that may be outdated.
