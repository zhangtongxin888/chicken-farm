Merge & Lucky Blocks

Chicken Farm Lucky Blocks & merging: three chickens in, one better out.

The Merge pad combines up to 3 chickens into one higher-quality bird. Lucky Blocks are the second way to get chickens: your chickens can lay them, and 3 working codes give one for free. Below you will find the pad layout, the reported Lucky Block types, and when opening a block is worth it.

Quick start

The farm pad map.

Early Chicken Farm is pad-based, not menu-based: you walk onto physical pads to do everything. Here is the full layout a beginner needs.

Pad What stepping on it does
Buy 1 / 5 / 25 / 100 Deducts cash and places that many chickens on your farm. Bigger pads are for wallets that can refill afterward.
Merge pad Combines up to 3 chickens into one higher-quality chicken.
Deposit Eggs Sends the eggs you collected to the Egg Shop for processing.
Brown board Not a pad — the board on the right side of the farm shows processing progress.
Collect Cash Hands you the cash from processed eggs. Feed it back into buys and merges.

Lucky Blocks

Chicken Farm Lucky Blocks: types, free blocks, timing.

Lucky Blocks are a real second source of chickens — with a catch that matters a lot in your first sessions.

How they work: every chicken has a chance to lay a Lucky Block, and each Lucky Block can reward one of three different chickens. Opening one can cost a small fee.

More block types over time: Update 7 added 2 new Lucky Blocks, and Updates 8 and 10 each added one more (game changelogs). The game does not publish drop tables or odds, so check them in-game.

Beginner advice: guides explicitly recommend skipping Lucky Blocks early. The fee plus a random result makes them a poor use of starter cash compared with guaranteed production chickens from the Buy pads.

When to use them: once your loop is stable and deposits keep cash flowing, Lucky Blocks become a fun way to chase index entries and bonus birds.

Merge FAQ

Merging questions, answered.

The details players ask about once the basic loop clicks.

How many chickens can I merge at once?

Up to 3 chickens at the Merge pad, combining into one higher-quality chicken.

Is there a full chicken tier list?

Not a verified one. Chicken counts and rarity details published before Update 10 (which added Gold Chickens) may already be stale, so this site does not print a tier tree. The in-game index is the reliable reference.

How do I get free Lucky Blocks in Chicken Farm?

Three working codes give one each: owemgeh, plsbesttier and 50MVisits (checked October 1, 2026). Your chickens can also lay Lucky Blocks while they produce. See the codes page for redeem steps.

What are Gold Chickens?

A new kind of chicken added in Update 10. The changelog does not explain how to get them, so watch your merge and Lucky Block results and check the in-game index. More on the Update 10 page.

Do Lucky Blocks replace buying chickens?

No. They are a random bonus source with an opening fee. The Buy pads give guaranteed chickens; Lucky Blocks add surprise ones on top once you can afford the gamble.

Should I merge or rebirth first?

Different systems: merging combines chickens into one higher-quality bird, while rebirth raises the cash multiplier and resets cash, eggs, chickens, and Processing Level. Review the exact reset rules on the rebirth page.
