The core loop

Every coin starts as an egg.

Whenever you feel stuck, come back to these four moves. They form the verified opening loop described by the approved sources.

1
🐔

Buy chickens

Step on the Buy 1, Buy 5, Buy 25, or Buy 100 pads. Every chicken you add starts laying eggs for your farm.

2
🥚

Collect & deposit eggs

Eggs roll out of the yellow chicken building. Walk over them to pick them up, then drop them on the Deposit Eggs pad and watch the brown board process them.

3
🪙

Collect the cash

Step on the Collect Cash pad and put the money straight back into more chickens and higher buy tiers.

4
✨

Merge for rarer birds

Combine up to 3 chickens at the Merge pad to unlock higher-quality chickens. Lucky Blocks can add new birds to the flock too.

Progression

Level up your farm in four stages.

Chicken Farm layers new systems on top of the loop as you grow. Here is the order that keeps early progress smooth.

LV.01

Learn the pads

Finish the short on-farm tutorial: collect eggs, deposit them, collect cash, buy a chicken, and try the Merge pad once.

LV.02

Scale the flock

Buy in bigger batches as cash allows, merge toward rarer chickens, and treat Lucky Blocks as extras once production is stable.

LV.03

Rebirth on purpose

Rebirth raises your cash multiplier and unlocks more Buy tiers — but a normal rebirth resets cash, eggs, chickens, and Processing Level. A Robux Skip advances without the reset.

LV.04

Spend gems

Gem Upgrades unlock at Rebirth 3. Update 10 added more of them, plus Gold Chickens, a new Lucky Block, a new rebirth, and 2 more Buy Tiers. The Double Gems pass is the only paid gem boost.

Update 10 details were checked on October 1, 2026. Always confirm costs inside the game.

Common mistakes

Four ways new farmers stall.

Most slow starts come from the same handful of habits. Avoid these and your farm keeps compounding.

✕

Buying Lucky Blocks first

Lucky Blocks cost a fee to open and pay out randomly. Beginner guides advise spending starter cash on production chickens instead.

✕

Ignoring rebirths forever

Never rebirthing leaves your egg-sale multiplier flat, which slows buying and merging into higher tiers.

✕

Hunting codes before the tutorial

The ABX codes control on the top HUD only becomes available after the short tutorial. Finish the tutorial first, then redeem.

✕

Copying codes from old lists

Old lists still include expired codes like woops and ctrlz, and the dead "chickfarm" Discord invite. Use the checked code list, which lists only codes three trackers agree are active.

Quick answers

Chicken Farm FAQ.

Short answers to the questions most likely to interrupt your first session. The full list lives on the FAQ page.

What should I do first in Chicken Farm?

Follow the in-game tutorial: collect eggs, deposit them, collect cash, then buy and merge a chicken. The same route is laid out step by step in the beginner guide.

Do chickens lay eggs while I am away?

Yes. The official description confirms chickens keep laying while you are away, so collect what piled up each time you return and feed it back into the loop.

How do I get rarer chickens?

Merge up to 3 chickens at the Merge pad to unlock higher-quality birds. Lucky Blocks are a second source of new chickens and help fill your index — details on the merge page.

How do I redeem codes?

After the tutorial, click the ABX button at the top-left of the HUD, paste the code exactly as shown, and press Redeem. As of October 1, 2026 there are 10 working codes, including update8, givemecash and toood. See the full list with rewards on the codes page.

Is there an official wiki or Trello for Chicken Farm?

No official wiki or Trello has been found. Skandi Studios posts news in the official Discord (discord.gg/chickenfarm) and the game description. This site is an independent Chicken Farm wiki that rechecks those sources, most recently on October 1, 2026.

What is new in Update 10?

Events, new Gem Upgrades, a new Lucky Block, Gold Chickens, an Obby, a new Rebirth, 2 new Buy Tiers, and balance changes. It went live on September 5, 2026. The Update 10 page explains what each change means for your farm.
