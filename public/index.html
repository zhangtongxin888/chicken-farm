Common mistakes

Three shortcuts that usually create confusion.

Good beginner advice is often about what to ignore. Keep your attention on actions the game itself confirms.

Avoid

Skipping collection and deposit

The tutorial teaches both for a reason: cash only helps progression after you bring production back into the loop.

Avoid

Rebirthing on autopilot

A rebirth is a reset decision. Read the current in-game summary before confirming instead of relying on an old table.

Avoid

Trusting unverified lists

Code lists, costs, and tier tables can change. This guide leaves them out when current evidence is not approved.

Quick answers

Chicken Farm FAQ.

Short answers to the questions most likely to interrupt your first session.

What should I do first in Chicken Farm?

Follow the tutorial actions: collect and deposit production, use the buy pad for eggs, then use the merge system. The complete route is in the beginner guide.

Does my farm have offline production?

Yes. Offline production is part of the game’s progression, so returning to your farm and collecting what it produced belongs in your routine.

When should I rebirth?

There is no universal moment that this guide can promise. Treat rebirth as a reset choice and review the current in-game summary before you confirm it.

Where is the current Chicken Farm codes list?

This guide does not publish a code list without approved current evidence. That keeps expired or invented codes from becoming part of your plan.

Are Lucky Blocks part of progression?

Lucky Blocks are a real side system in Chicken Farm. Use them as part of your wider progression, after you understand the main buy, merge, collect, and reinvest loop.
