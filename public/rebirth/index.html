Quick start

What rebirth actually does.

Rebirths raise your cash multiplier and unlock extra Buy tiers and items — guides describe them as the way to keep improving your earnings. Here is the safe sequence.

Meet the cash requirement

Open the Rebirth tab. The cash rebirth button only becomes available — shown in red — once your current cash meets the requirement. A grey button means you are not there yet.

Read the reset summary

A normal rebirth is a run reset, not an account wipe — but it clears cash, eggs, chickens, and Processing Level. Confirm you are ready to rebuild before pressing it.

Choose: normal rebirth or Robux Skip

The Rebirth menu also offers a Robux Skip, which advances to the next rebirth while keeping your cash, chickens, eggs, and Processing Level. Normal rebirth is free; Skip costs Robux.

Rebuild on a higher multiplier

After the reset, continue with the higher cash multiplier and the extra Buy tiers or items described by approved guides. Restart the core loop: buy, collect, deposit, cash out, merge.

Two ways to get rebirth wrong.

Both extremes hurt. The middle path is boring and effective.

Never rebirthing

Approved guides say skipping rebirths leaves the egg-sale multiplier flat, which slows buying and merging higher-tier chickens. Check the live requirement before choosing a normal rebirth.

Because a normal rebirth wipes cash, eggs, chickens, and Processing Level, review those four reset items before confirming. The Robux Skip keeps those four items.

Rebirth FAQ

Before you press the red button.

Short answers for the most common reset worries.

Does rebirth wipe my whole account?

No. Guides describe it as a run reset: cash, eggs, chickens, and Processing Level reset, while rebirth raises the cash multiplier and can unlock extra Buy tiers or items. A Robux Skip advances while keeping cash, eggs, chickens, and Processing Level.

What is Processing Level?

A named progression stat tied to egg processing. Approved sources say it is wiped by a normal rebirth and kept when you use the Robux Skip.

When do Gem Upgrades unlock?

At Rebirth 3, per the official Update 7 changelog checked August 20, 2026. Gem costs and effects are not documented outside the game.

What is the current rebirth cap and cost list?

We do not publish one. Pre-Update-7 community tables conflict, and Update 7 added two new rebirths — the only reliable list is the in-game rebirth screen on the day you play.

What does the Robux Skip retain?

The approved distinction is exact: the Robux Skip keeps cash, chickens, eggs, and Processing Level, while a normal rebirth resets those four items. Confirm the live Robux price and normal-rebirth requirement in the game.
